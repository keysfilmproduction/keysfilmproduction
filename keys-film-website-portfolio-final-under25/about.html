WHO WE ARE

Key's Film Production & Academy

Key's Film Production & Academy is a top-rated event management and media production company, originally established in Mumbai in 2015, with a strong operational presence in Indore.

We are known for delivering creative, high-quality and result-driven event and branding solutions across Mumbai and Indore. From planning and production to execution, our team brings ideas to life with creativity, precision and professionalism.

We also operate as a modeling institute and casting academy, providing complete support for talent management and media production.
