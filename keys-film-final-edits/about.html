ABOUT KEYS

Keys Film Production & Event Management Company

Key's Film Production & Academy is a top-rated event management and media production company, originally established in Mumbai in the year 2015, with a strong operational presence in Indore. We are known for delivering creative, high-quality and result-driven event and branding solutions across Mumbai and Indore. We specialize in wedding planning, corporate events, exhibition management, exhibition stall design, film production, line production, professional photoshoots, product shoots, advertisement shoots, model shoots, casting services, movie promotions and on-road branding activities etc. We also operate as a modeling institute and casting academy, providing complete support for talent management and media production.
